# Visual system: Field Notes

The carousel reads as the working notes of a design studio's research project, not as an app advert. They sit on the StudioLLM profile ("R&D by machines") next to the studio's other projects, so the title treatment matches those posts.

## Principles

- **Show the material, then the method.** Every slide is a figure, a table or a short note. No buttons, pills, check icons or drawn app screens.
- **Number everything.** Figures carry `Fig.` captions, method steps are numbered `01` to `04`, slides carry a page number. Captions say what the image is and how it was made.
- **State limits plainly.** "Base development, no manual edits." "Plain inversion leaves a blue cast." No hype and no invented numbers. Any mention of AI upscaling must say that it can invent detail.
- **Photo pages inside the carousel.** Full pages of plates and details sit between the notes and break up the reading.

## Grammar

- **Canvas and grid:** every slide is 1080x1440 (3:4) and uses the same grid. Margin 64 px on both sides, on photo pages too. Content starts at the cap height of the title (y 151) and ends on a common bottom line (y 1308). The header and footer sit in the same place on every slide.
- **Frame:** a header with `StudioLLM / Ai-Film-Developing` on the left and the section on the right, over a hairline. A footer with the credit on the left and `01 / 13` on the right, under a hairline. Nothing crosses those lines.
- **Backgrounds:** near-black `#0E0F11` for photographs, light grey `#F3F3F1` for method and tables. No paper or kraft textures.
- **Colour:** grey for the first part of each title, cobalt `#2E5BFF` for the second part, as on the studio's other posts. Cobalt also marks section labels, figure boxes and the developed curve. On dark slides small blue text uses `#7C98FF`. Never use orange, terracotta or coral as a UI colour; the orange in the photos is the film itself.
- **Type:**
  - Titles: Instrument Sans Bold 92 px on every slide, tracking -4.5%, line height 0.98, two lines, ending with a full stop. 40 px from title to content.
  - Body: Instrument Sans Regular, 29 to 40 px.
  - Metadata, captions, labels, credits, addresses: IBM Plex Mono, 19 to 24 px.
- **Figures:** numbered Fig. 1 to Fig. 10 through the carousel, every one with a caption 24 px below it. Photo pages have no title: the grid starts where the titles start and its caption sits on the common bottom line. Photographs have square corners and registration marks at the corners. A negative and its development are always the same frame at the same size. A side-by-side comparison is split by a thin white line; `a`, `b`, `c` tags name the panels.
- **Swipe cue:** a quiet `next →` in mono on the cover.

## Contrast

Text meets WCAG AA. On dark slides captions are 7.6:1 and small blue text 7.4:1. On light slides captions are 5.9:1 and blue text 4.6:1. The grey first part of each title is set at display size (92 px and up), where the AA limit for large text is 3:1.
