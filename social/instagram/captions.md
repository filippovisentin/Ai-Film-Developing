## Field notes · Ai-Film-Developing

**Caption**

Field notes from a StudioLLM project: developing film negatives with a phone and a browser.

01 Capture. Photograph the negative on a light pad or a white screen, with some film edge in the frame.
02 Profile. Choose the film stock, or the generic profile.
03 Inversion. The engine estimates the film base from the frame edges, then inverts and balances. A plain inversion is not enough: the orange base turns into a blue cast.
04 Output. Adjust if needed, then save a JPEG or a 16-bit TIFF.

Between the notes: six glass plates from the Kodak factory in Abbotsford, Melbourne, c. 1930s, as scanned and after base development, with details side by side. No manual edits.

It runs in the browser, so the photo stays on your device. Free, open source, no account. Link in bio.

Sources. Kodak Ektar 100 strip: LoMit, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons. Fuji Superia 200: Florian Schott, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons (slide 09 is shared under CC BY-SA 3.0). Glass negatives: Museums Victoria, public domain. All developed with the engine.
